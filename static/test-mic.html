JanAwaz mic self-test (prototype demo)

Press the button and say in Hindi: "Hamare mohalle mein teen din se paani nahi aa raha."

Result will appear here.

This page tests only whether this browser can recognize Hindi speech. It sends audio to the browser's own speech service and saves nothing. Needs HTTPS or localhost, and Chrome or Edge. Recognition depends on the browser, its speech service and mic permission: in our tests, Chrome 157 on a Linux build refused with a permission/service error, while browsers on Android phones and on desktop Windows or Mac generally ship a speech service. Any failure here means the main app uses its typed fallback by design, not that the product fails.
